# SEO audit — deltaenergysolution.com — 13 September 2026

Published report (same findings, designed for reading):
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/75891326-0e74-4a46-8112-93c9132c4385

Companion playbook for AI search and listings: `docs/ai-search-listing.md`.

Everything below was measured from the live site on the day it launched.
Nothing is estimated from memory. Method and evidence are at the end.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Findable, readable by machines, and not yet trusted.**

Technically this is a strong site. Every route is prerendered, so crawlers
that do not run JavaScript, which is all of the AI ones, receive the full
text. Titles and descriptions are unique and within length. Headings are
clean. Every image has alt text. A ten-node schema.org graph sits on every
page with the page node and the FAQ block swapped per route. The `llms.txt`
is better than most agencies ship. A CI gate re-checks all of it on every
deploy.

What is missing is the layer above that: everything a search engine or an
answer engine uses to decide whether Delta is a real, credible entity.
Google cannot connect the site to a Google Business Profile because nothing
links to one. The postcode on the site disagrees with the one on the
listing. There are no reviews, no social profiles, no founders in the data,
and the About page publishes an invented timeline. A second product,
docsun, lives on the same domain at `app.` with its own indexed calculator,
so the domain currently represents two businesses. And there is no
analytics or Search Console, so none of this can be measured.

Most of the high-impact fixes are small. The ones that need real material,
photographs and real numbers, are the ones only Delta can supply.

`valid-source-maps` (best practices, cosmetic). SEO category: none.

### What the numbers mean

On every page measured, the largest contentful element is a **paragraph of
text**: the hero subtitle on the homepage, the lead paragraph elsewhere.
It paints late for two reasons.

1. **Render-blocking stylesheets and four font files** (77 kB) cost 2.1 s on
   the homepage and about 1 s on the others before anything can draw.
2. **The entrance animation holds that text invisible** until the 208 kB of
   scripts (540 kB uncompressed, one bundle) have loaded and run. The
   page's most important words are the last thing to appear. This is the
   `.hero__rise` / `.reveal` pattern: elements start at `opacity: 0` and a
   GSAP ScrollTrigger sets `data-in` when the bundle executes.

The 3.3 MB `hero.mp4` is **not** the LCP element, but it is three quarters
of the homepage's 4.5 MB transfer and competes for the same slow
connection. It is the difference between 51 on the homepage and 68 on the
video-free pages. Homepage layout shift (0.096) sits just under the 0.1
threshold and will cross it on a slower device.

### Fix

- Let above-the-fold text be visible at rest. Animate only what is below
  the fold, or animate from a visible resting state (translate/opacity
  from 1). This alone moves LCP from six seconds to when the fonts land.
- `<link rel="preload" as="font">` for the two Switzer files used above
  the fold; `font-display: swap` if not already set.
- `preload="none"` on the video and start it after first paint (an
  `IntersectionObserver` or `requestIdleCallback`), or replace it with a
  still and a short clip under 1 MB (the image brief already asks for
  this).
- Code-split: `React.lazy` the Calculator and load GSAP only on routes that
  animate, so text-only pages stop paying for both.

## 3. What is already right

Worth naming, because it is the reason the rest is fixable rather than a
rebuild.

- Prerendered HTML on all 13 routes: non-JS crawlers see 700–1,200 words per
  page, not an empty `<div>`.
- Unique title and description per page, 51–62 and 127–163 characters.
- Exactly one H1 per page, no heading levels skipped, every H1 names the
  service and mostly the place.
- Every image has an `alt` attribute; logos and portraits are correctly
  marked decorative (`alt=""`).
- Canonical, `og:url` and sitemap agree on every page. `www` and `http`
  redirect to the apex.
- Robots meta `index, follow, max-image-preview:large, max-snippet:-1,
  max-video-preview:-1` on every page.
- Schema.org graph on every page: Organization, WebSite, WebPage,
  LocalBusiness + HomeAndConstructionBusiness, five Service nodes, FAQPage.
  WebPage and FAQPage are per-route (verified on `/`, `/subsidy/`,
  `/services/hybrid/`, `/contact/`).
- LocalBusiness carries real phone, email, address, opening hours, GSTIN as
  `taxID`, `hasMap`, eight `brand` entries and a price-tier
  `hasOfferCatalog`.
- 65 question-and-answer pairs across 13 sets, one set per route, each in
  FAQPage schema.
- `llms.txt`, 117 lines: pages, services, prices before and after subsidy,
  planning assumptions, the full FAQ, real contact details.
- `robots.txt` names 12 AI and answer-engine crawlers and allows each;
  blocks four bandwidth scrapers; points at the sitemap.
- `sitemap.xml` with priorities and lastmod; `humans.txt`; web manifest;
  full favicon set; `og.jpg` 1200×630 designed card.
- HTTPS on its own certificate, immutable caching for hashed assets,
  `no-cache` HTML, dotfiles denied.
- `scripts/seo-check.mjs` asserts head tags, headings, JSON-LD, alt text and
  assets on every deploy.

## 4. The gaps, in the order to fix them

Ranked by cost to search and answer-engine visibility, weighed against
effort. *Small* means an afternoon in this repo. *Medium* means it needs
material from Delta or a change in the ERP repo.

### 4.1 Every unknown URL returns 200 with the homepage — Critical, Small

**Evidence.** `curl -sI https://deltaenergysolution.com/this-page-does-not-exist/`
→ `200`, body is the homepage (`<title>Rooftop Solar in Malappuram…`).

**Why it matters.** Google treats this as a soft 404: it wastes crawl
budget, can index junk URLs (anything anyone links or mistypes), and is a
site-quality signal. Every real route is prerendered, so the single-page
fallback that causes it is not needed.

**Fix.**
1. Add a prerendered `404.html` (a route in `scripts/prerender.mjs` or a
   static page in `public/`) with the nav, a short line, and links to the
   five most useful pages. `noindex` it.
2. In `deploy/nginx/deltasite.conf.template`, apex `:443` block:
   `try_files $uri $uri/ $uri/index.html =404;` and `error_page 404 /404.html;`
3. Tell the Delta-MVP session to re-copy the template (it copies byte for
   byte; the hash moves).
4. Add to `seo-check.mjs`: fetch a random path on the built site and assert
   404 (or assert `dist/404.html` exists and the template has `=404`).

### 4.2 Nothing is measured — Critical, Small

**Evidence.** No `gtag`, `googletagmanager`, Plausible, Umami, Clarity or
Matomo script on any page. No Search Console verification token or DNS
record known.

**Fix.**
1. **Google Search Console**: verify the domain property via DNS TXT at
   Hostinger (covers apex, www and all subdomains). Submit
   `https://deltaenergysolution.com/sitemap.xml`. Request indexing for
   the 13 URLs once.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: import from Search Console (one click). Bing
   feeds Copilot, ChatGPT search, DuckDuckGo and Perplexity's fallback.
3. **IndexNow**: generate a key, place `public/<key>.txt`, and add a step to
   `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` that POSTs the sitemap URLs to
   `https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow` after the smoke test. Bing and
   partners re-crawl within minutes instead of days. Google ignores
   IndexNow; the sitemap covers Google.
4. **Analytics**: Plausible (no cookie banner needed) or GA4. Three custom
   events: `call` on `tel:` clicks, `whatsapp` on `wa.me` clicks, `quote`
   on form submit. Add outbound-link tracking so referrers like
   `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai`, `copilot.microsoft.com` and
   `gemini.google.com` are visible as AI traffic.

### 4.3 The contact form throws every lead away — Critical, Small

**Evidence.** `src/components/Contact.jsx`: `submit()` validates name and
phone, sets `sent = true`, renders "We've got your details and will call
you back within 24 hours." No request is made.

**Fix.** Until the ERP lead API exists (`docs/lead-capture-api.md`), build a
`wa.me/917510500080?text=` URL from the fields on submit and open it. The
lead lands in the channel Delta already answers. Keep the success state.
Track it as the `quote` event.

### 4.4 Google cannot tie the site to the business — High, Small

**Evidence.** Organization `sameAs` absent (`CONTACT.sameAs: []`).
LocalBusiness has no `geo`. Postcode `676519` on site vs `676505` on the
Google Business Profile (noted in `site.config.js:57-65`). No reviews on
the site, no `aggregateRating`. No social profile anywhere.

**Why it matters.** The local pack (map results for "solar installer
Malappuram") and Google's AI answers for local queries are driven by the
Business Profile, review count and rating, and NAP consistency. Answer
engines use `sameAs` and external listings to confirm an entity exists.

**Fix.**
1. Reconcile the postcode with the client; the site and GBP must match.
2. `CONTACT.sameAs`: GBP share URL, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn
   (create the ones that do not exist, with the identical business name),
   Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART pages, the PM Surya Ghar vendor listing,
   any ANERT/KSEB empanelment page. `gen-seo.mjs` already emits `sameAs`
   when non-empty; `seo-check.mjs` already warns when empty, turn that into
   a launch FAIL.
3. `geo`: `GeoCoordinates` from the map pin, added in `gen-seo.mjs`.
4. `contactPoint` with `contactType: "sales"`, telephone, `areaServed`,
   `availableLanguage: ["en", "ml"]`.
5. Reviews: after every commissioning, send the GBP review link on
   WhatsApp. Once three or more exist, show them on the homepage with the
   reviewer's first name and town (with consent). Note: Google does not
   show rich-result stars for self-serving reviews on a LocalBusiness's own
   site; the value is for humans and AI readers, not the star snippet.

### 4.5 A second business shares the domain — High, Small–Medium

**Evidence.** `https://app.deltaenergysolution.com/` is indexed, titled
"docsun — Solar paperwork, simplified", with a sitemap listing `/`,
`/solar-savings-calculator`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, and a robots.txt that
allows crawling. It is a software product, not the installer.

**Why it matters.** Search engines resolve a domain to one entity. Today
the domain says Delta is both a rooftop-solar installer and a SaaS vendor.
Two calculators on one domain compete for the same query, and the docsun
landing page can outrank the apex for brand searches.

**Fix.** Long term, docsun gets its own domain. Short term, in the Delta-MVP
repo: `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` on `/`, `/privacy`,
`/terms` and `/solar-savings-calculator` of the app frontend, remove the
app's sitemap from its robots.txt, and 301 `/solar-savings-calculator` to
`https://deltaenergysolution.com/savings-calculator/`. The ERP itself is
unaffected either way.

### 4.6 The About page publishes invented facts — High, Medium

**Evidence.** `content/about.js` `isPlaceholder: true`; the milestone
timeline (2020, 2022, 2024, 2026), the credentials list and the homepage
"numbers" are placeholders. Live under `SEO_ALLOW_PLACEHOLDER`, by the
owner's decision. Founders are named on the page but absent from the
schema.

**Why it matters.** Search engines weigh experience, expertise and trust.
A fabricated history is worse than none, and it is a legal exposure under
the business's own name. The real trust signals are unused: two named
founders who are electrical engineers, a B-Class electrical contractor
licence, an MNRE registration, founding year 2017.

**Fix.**
1. Replace the timeline and credentials with real data, or delete the two
   sections and the invented figures. Flip `isPlaceholder` to false and
   remove `SEO_ALLOW_PLACEHOLDER` from the workflow.
2. `gen-seo.mjs`: emit both founders as `Person` nodes (`name`,
   `jobTitle`, `worksFor`, `hasCredential` for the B-Class licence),
   referenced from Organization `founder`.
3. Publish the MNRE registration number and the licence number on the
   About page.
4. Homepage stats: replace the generic subsidy/payback tiles with real
   figures: installations completed, total kW, years, districts.

### 4.7 Stock photographs captioned as Delta's own work — High, Medium

**Evidence.** `/projects/` shows four Unsplash photos over captions such as
"3 kW · Residential · Manjeri". All five service images and nine homepage
images are hotlinked from `images.unsplash.com`. `hero.mp4` (3.3 MB) and
its poster are wind-turbine footage. Zero images on the site are of
Delta's work. See `docs/image-brief.md`.

**Fix.** Photograph four real installations with the customers' consent;
publish fewer cards if there are fewer photos. Replace the hero with a still
or a short clip of a Delta rooftop. Self-host every image as WebP with
`srcset` widths; add image entries to the sitemap. Add `ImageObject` with
`creditText`/`copyrightHolder` Delta where the photos are Delta's.

### 4.8 The geography is inconsistent, and Kozhikode has no page — High, Medium

**Evidence.** `SITE.title`, most H1s, the meta description, `llms.txt` and
`areaServed` (`Malappuram`, `Kerala`) all say Malappuram. `/about/` and
`/projects/` now say "across Kerala" and name Kozhikode. The four towns in
the copy (Manjeri, Kottakkal, Tirur, Perinthalmanna) are not in the
schema. No page targets Kozhikode.

**Fix.**
1. Decide: Malappuram and Kozhikode as primary markets, Kerala secondary.
2. `AREA.towns` + Kozhikode into `areaServed` as `City` nodes
   (`gen-seo.mjs`).
3. Two genuine city pages, `/solar-malappuram/` and `/solar-kozhikode/`,
   each with real local detail: KSEB section offices, typical roof types,
   real installs in that district, distance and service promise. Not a
   templated set; two thin doorway pages would hurt more than help.
4. Align `SITE.title`/description once the decision is made.

### 4.9 One share image for all thirteen pages — Medium, Small

**Evidence.** Every page's `og:image` and `twitter:image` is `/og.jpg`.
The card reads "Rooftop solar in Malappuram." regardless of the page.

**Why it matters.** In Kerala, links travel on WhatsApp, and the card is
the pitch. Sharing the subsidy page should show the subsidy.

**Fix.** `scripts/og-images.mjs` already renders the card from a template
with headless Chrome. Loop over `site.routes.js`, render one card per route
with that page's headline and one figure, write `public/og/<slug>.jpg`,
carry `ogImage` per route, and update `checkHead` in `seo-check.mjs` to
assert the per-route value.

### 4.10 Thirteen pages is the whole site — Medium, ongoing

**Evidence.** 13 URLs in the sitemap. No pricing page although prices are
in `site.config.js` and `llms.txt`. No guides. Nothing in Malayalam. The
competitor studied in `research/illumine/` has 95 URLs including a blog
and a "3 kW solar panel price in Kerala with subsidy" page.

**Fix.**
1. `/solar-panel-price-kerala/`: the price table by kW (before and after
   subsidy), what is included, what moves the price, FAQ, `Offer` schema.
   Built entirely from `SYSTEM_PRICES` and `subsidyFor()` so it cannot
   drift from the calculator.
2. One dated guide a month from the FAQ material: KSEB net metering rules
   as they change, subsidy changes, reading a KSEB bill, choosing panel
   brands. Each with author byline (a founder), date, sources.
3. Malayalam versions of `/`, `/subsidy/` and `/services/residential/`
   under `/ml/…` with `hreflang` pairs. The prerender already handles new
   routes.

### 4.11 Structured data stops short — Medium, Small

**Missing today.** `BreadcrumbList`; `founder`/`Person`; `geo`;
`contactPoint`; `offers` on `Service` (prices are public); `dateModified`
on `WebPage`; per-route `lastmod` (sitemap uses the build date for every
URL, which makes it meaningless); towns in `areaServed`.

**Fix.** In `gen-seo.mjs` and `scripts/prerender.mjs`: add
`BreadcrumbList` per route (and a visible breadcrumb on service detail
pages); `Person` founders; `GeoCoordinates`; `ContactPoint`; an `Offer`
per Service from `SYSTEM_PRICES`; an `updated` field per route in
`site.routes.js` emitted as `dateModified` and as sitemap `lastmod`.
Extend `seo-check.mjs` to assert each.

### 4.12 Internal links are the same thirteen on every page — Medium, Small

**Evidence.** Every page links to exactly the 13 nav/footer targets. No
body copy links the subsidy page to the calculator, residential to net
metering, or a FAQ answer to the page that expands on it.

**Fix.** Let `content/*.js` body paragraphs carry links (a tiny markdown
subset or `[text](path)` in `Prose.jsx`). Add two or three contextual links
per page with descriptive anchors, and a "Related" strip on service detail
pages.

### 4.13 Smaller items — Low, Small

- No `srcset` or modern formats on any image. Brand logos are 62–66 kB
  PNGs; one partner logo is already SVG.
- No security headers (`Strict-Transport-Security`,
  `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`) on the apex; the ERP hosts
  set them. Add to the nginx template when it is next edited.
- `/contact/` is 235 words; add what to have ready, how a site visit goes,
  service hours by area.
- No `twitter:site` because no account exists.
- Sitemap lists one image; list the real ones once they exist.
- Lighthouse `color-contrast`: a few muted-on-paper text pairs; check
  `--mute` on `--paper-2`.

## 9. AI answer engines

What they can read today: full text on every route; 65 direct Q&A pairs in
FAQPage schema; `llms.txt` with prices and assumptions; explicit robots
permission for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot,
PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Amazonbot, Bingbot,
CCBot; sourced claims (KSEB portal cited).

What they cannot resolve: who Delta is (no `sameAs`, founders, reviews or
external listing; docsun on the same domain); where exactly (Malappuram vs
Kerala vs Kozhikode); how big and how long (no install count or kW, an
invented timeline); when the text was last true (no dates); what the work
looks like (no photographs of Delta's own installations).

The full playbook, including which engine uses which index, the entity
kit, IndexNow, and content shape for citation, is in
`docs/ai-search-listing.md`.

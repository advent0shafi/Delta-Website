Abad Fisheries is one of the largest processors of quality quick frozen seafood in India with 11 accredited and certified factories PAN India.The Director of Abad Fisheries Mr. Anwar Hashim, decided to adopt energy efficient measures and Renewable Energy as an alternative option for their Bangalore facility.

ILLUMINE carried out an energy audit at site and implemented energy efficient measures, designed and implemented a 100kWp Solar Grid Tie Project. The project is capable of supporting Deep freezers and covers 95% of there load requirements.

The Project is done using SMA German manufactured Inverters and ReneSola Modules – which are used in CIAL – Cochin Airport for 12 MW project. All the approvals from BESCOM and PPA is signed for the project.It is expectedto generate 40 lakhs Units of Electricity during its lifetime and ensures a payback within 5 years.

The Project is generating 500 units/day with grid export facility and live data monitoring, helping the customer to monitor day to day generation.

Mr. Anwar Hashim (MD, ABAD Fisheries)

Envirodesigns Eco Labs is an organization mainly engaged in the area of General Testing in Chemical and Biological Parameters, Quality Assessment and Environmental Consultancy Services. The head office is located at PalarivattomErnakulam near Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium.

As their power consumption kept increased on account of increased business activities, reliability on grid supply was no longer desirable. Furthermore, the higher tariff and issues with KSEB added the cost.

Our Mission was to clear the issues with KSEB and implement a reliable On Grid solution at the available space considering maximum utility.

Team ILLUMINE implemented a 20 kWpOn grid project using Canadian Solar Modules and SMA Germany Inverters. The system produces 105 Units of electricity per day and covers almost 75% of the power requirements. And saves 40 tons of Co2 emission into atmosphere.

Mr. K L Antony (MD, Envirodesigns-Eco Tower)

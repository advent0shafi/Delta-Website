HYBRID SOLAR SYSTEM PROJECT

A hybrid solar system is a combination of the advantages of the ON Grid and OFF Grid Projects. Here the hybrid inverter intelligently switches between using solar power, battery storage, and grid power.

Who needs to install a hybrid solar project ?

Hybrid Projects are suitable for customers who want to reduce their electricity bill at the same time need backup and need a solution for power fluctuation with intelligent power management.

Hybrid solar system working

Here during the daytime solar hybrid inverter converts DC power from panels to AC power and feeds live load at Home/industry. After meeting this requirement excess power is used to charge the battery. Once both of these requirements are met, excess power will be exported to Grid(KSEB/BESCOM) – like in the ON grid project. During the night Grid power will be utilized until a power failure/backup requirement comes. Also whenever there comes a power fluctuation or low voltage etc, the hybrid utilises the solar power saved in batteries to run the load.

Unlike the ON grid, when the Grid power goes off, hybrid inverter won't shut down completely. Whereas it just stops grid export and utilizes solar power for live load and battery charging.

Hybrid solar installation advantages

With the hybrid solar project, the customer has the power and advantages of both On-grid and Off-grid. Hybrid Systems are recommended for NRI and Professionals. As here even when the customers are not at home, the power generated won’t go wasted as it gets exported to the grid. Also, your power will be stable, even when your grid power is fluctuating or unstable.

Best hybrid inverters in India

When choosing hybrid inverters, it is best to choose one with a long warranty and credible market feedback. Also, it has to provide necessary safety features as the solar hybrid inverter will be active, even when the grid fails.

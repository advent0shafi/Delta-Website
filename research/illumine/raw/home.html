FAQs

Answers to the most frequently asked questions.

What kind of solar services does Illumine Energy provide?

Illumine Energy offers comprehensive solar solutions, including on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid systems, as well as maintenance and consultations. As part of our services, we also provide net metering support and annual maintenance contracts (AMCs).

Does Illumine Energy help with solar subsidies?

Absolutely. We assist customers in obtaining government solar subsidies. Our team provides support with MNRE and state-level application and approval processes. Subsidies are credited directly to the customer's bank account following installation.

How can I request a solar consultation or quote online?

Please fill out the Enquiry form on our website with your contact information and project details to request a consultation. We will contact you to schedule a site visit or provide a quote tailored to your specific requirements.

Why should I choose Illumine Energy over other providers?

Providing end-to-end solar solutions, Illumine Energy is focused on quality, reliability, and long-term value. As a solar investment firm with over 300 installations and years of experience in India and the UAE, we provide expert guidance, reliable service, and profitable returns on investments.

Where is Illumine Energy located?

Illumine Energy is headquartered in Ernakulam, Kerala, with its registered office at 2nd Floor, SN Complex, Koonamthai Junction, Edappally, Ernakulam – 682024, Kerala, India.

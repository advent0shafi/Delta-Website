A leading solar energy company

Illumine Energy undertakes designing, engineering, product procurement, documentation, installation and maintenace of power plants. Unlike product sales, company is unique in focusing project sales, by Energy Auditing, Turn Key Solutions and Helping Customer to do Profitable Investments.


Established in the year 2012, Illumine is a top player in the category development, acquisition and long-term management of large-scale solar projects and smart energy solutions. With one of the largest footprints of clean energy projects, spanning India, the company has installed more than 5000+ solar power projects and has the largest portfolio of solar rooftops. The company has over 10 MW of solar power plants under operation and a further 2 KW under construction.


We operate in both the centralized energy market and the evolving distributed energy market around the India and UAE. ILLUMINE is investing in new technologies and product offerings from the advantageous position of pure solar economics.

Illumine undertakes complete EPC of solar projects including

Planning, conceptualization and documentation of power plants

Design, engineering and preparation of detailed project report

Procurement of components

Commissioning, operation and maintenance with performance guarantee

  <H1> MICRO INVERTERS
  Micro inverters are devices used with solar arrays to convert the energy that is generated to usable electricity for a home. In Microinverters each panel is connected to single microinverters. So the Advantages are; 1 ) The ability for each panel to work individually using microinverters, however, allows for greater energy production and efficiency - especially when only a few panels are in the shade or under snow. 2 ) Microinverters are recommended on most home solar projects since most roofs have some sort of shading and also it removes the necessity for a separate inverter. 3 ) Even though it possesses a high initial cost, it can be justified in cases where ther e are technical challenges in installing a series of solar panels or shading issues that could cause the entire system to underperform. 4 ) Micro inverters have a longer life compared to ordinary inverters (20-25 Years). 5 ) Micro inverters also have 95% maximum efficiency like string inverters even though they are a newer technology. 6 ) Another factor weighing in favor of the micro inverter market is that microinverters are much safer than central inverters since microinverters convert the electricity to AC at the panel level.

  <H3> ENPHASE Micro inverters
  Enphase microinverters are proven to produce more energy than other technologies.
  • Microinverters operate independently, so each panel performs to its fullest. One shaded or dirty panel won't affect the rest of the system.
  • Burst technology lets microinverters produce more at dawn, dusk, and in low-light conditions, for a longer solar day.
  • Enphase microinverters come with a 10-year warranty; string inverters need to be replaced at least once in the lifetime of the system.

  <H3> Residential Solar Projects
  When choosing a solar system in your home, The first thing of concern is how much plant capacity will be apt for your home to meet the daily consumption. To calculate that we have to know the daily/Bimonthly unit consumption or The approximate bimonthly electricity bill amount from the utility grid(KSEB/BESCOM). According to the solar design aspects, 4-5 units of electricity can be generated from a 1kW system per day.

The second thing is the area of your home in which the solar system will accommodate. You Can take advice from a technically strong person from electrical background or demand a site visit from a concerned person from any solar EPC company.

  <H1> HYBRID SOLAR SYSTEM PROJECT
  A hybrid solar system is a combination of the advantages of the ON Grid and OFF Grid Projects. Here the hybrid inverter intelligently switches between using solar power, battery storage, and grid power.
  <H5> Who needs to install a hybrid solar project ?
  Hybrid Projects are suitable for customers who want to reduce their electricity bill at the same time need backup and need a solution for power fluctuation with intelligent power management.

--- SECTION 5  id=  group=""  class="simple-services-area section-gap" ---
  <H3> Hybrid solar system working
  Here during the daytime solar hybrid inverter converts DC power from panels to AC power and feeds live load at Home/industry. After meeting this requirement excess power is used to charge the battery. Once both of these requirements are met, excess power will be exported to Grid(KSEB/BESCOM) – like in the ON grid project. During the night Grid power will be utilized until a power failure/backup requirement comes. Also whenever there comes a power fluctuation or low voltage etc, the hybrid utilises the solar power saved in batteries to run the load. Unlike the ON grid, when the Grid power goes off, hybrid inverter won't shut down completely. Whereas it just stops grid export and utilizes solar power for live load and battery charging.

--- SECTION 6  id=  group=""  class="callaction-area section-gap" ---
  <H2> Detailed working of On grid power plant
  During daytime when solar electricity is generated by the Grid Tie project, inverter checks whether the customer has a power requirement at home/building/industry to which the On grid project is installed. And on identifying power requirements at that instant, electricity generated( it is the AC electricity, which is converted by the inverter from the DC Electricity produced by the solar panels) will be given to that particular building to meet the consumption. After meeting the specific power requirement, the rest of electricity generated will be exported to the grid. Which will be measured by a bidirectional energy meter. During the night, when the sun goes and the customer has a power requirement, that will be met by the utility power grid. This power consumed or imported from the grid will be measured by the same bidirectional energy meter.
